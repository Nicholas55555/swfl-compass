# Hurricane Preparation Guide for Renters in Lee County

Living in a rental property in Lee County requires proactive communication with your landlord and a clear understanding of your specific evacuation zone. This guide outlines the essential steps to protect yourself, your belongings, and your legal rights during hurricane season.

## 1. Know Your Risks and Rights
*   **Identify your zone:** Visit the [Lee County Evacuation Zone Map](https://www.leegov.com/hurricane/storm/evacuation) to see if you are in a mandatory evacuation area.
*   **Review your lease:** Check your lease agreement for clauses regarding storm preparation, window protection (shutters), and renter's insurance requirements.
*   **Get Renter's Insurance:** Landlord insurance **does not** cover your personal belongings. Ensure you have a policy that includes flood coverage, as standard policies often exclude it.

## 2. Pre-Storm Checklist
- [ ] **Document everything:** Take photos or videos of every room and all personal belongings for insurance purposes.
- [ ] **Communicate with your landlord:** Ask if the property has hurricane shutters and who is responsible for installing them. If you are responsible, ensure you know how to operate them.
- [ ] **Secure outdoor items:** If you have a patio or balcony, you are responsible for bringing all furniture, plants, and decor inside before a storm.
- [ ] **Emergency Kit:** Prepare a "Go-Bag" with important documents (lease, insurance policy, ID, medical records) in a waterproof container.
- [ ] **Sign up for alerts:** Register for [AlertLee](https://www.leegov.com/publicsafety/emergencymanagement/alertlee) to receive emergency notifications via text, email, or phone.

## 3. During and After the Storm
1.  **Follow evacuation orders:** If your zone is ordered to evacuate, leave immediately. Do not stay in a rental unit that is not structurally sound or is in a flood-prone area.
2.  **Report damage:** If the property is damaged, notify your landlord in writing as soon as it is safe to do so. Take photos of the damage before any cleanup begins.
3.  **Document repairs:** Keep a log of all communications with your landlord regarding repairs and save receipts for any emergency supplies or temporary housing costs.
