# Sunny Days & Wild Ways: A 3-Day Fort Myers Family Escape

Welcome to Fort Myers! March is a fantastic time to visit—the weather is typically mild and dry, perfect for outdoor exploration. This itinerary balances relaxing beach time with unique Southwest Florida nature experiences that kids will love. Having a rental car is highly recommended for getting around the region easily.

---

## Day 1: Coastal Wonders
**Morning:** [J.N. "Ding" Darling National Wildlife Refuge](https://maps.google.com/?cid=5526802717624476472&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA) (Sanibel). Explore this world-famous refuge via the 4-mile Wildlife Drive. It’s excellent for spotting birds, alligators, and sometimes manatees. ($)

**Afternoon:** [Fort Myers Beach](https://maps.google.com/?cid=12248172002938270226&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA). Head back to the mainland for classic white-sand fun. The area is bustling with family-friendly activities and easy beach access. (Free)

**Evening:** Dinner in the Fort Myers Beach area to enjoy the sunset over the Gulf.

## Day 2: Nature & Discovery
**Morning:** [Six Mile Cypress Slough Preserve](https://maps.google.com/?cid=15531174903539345209&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA). Walk the 1.2-mile elevated boardwalk through protected wetlands. It’s stroller-friendly, shaded, and great for spotting turtles and wading birds. (Free)

**Afternoon:** [Wonder Gardens](https://maps.google.com/?cid=4335061881420675794&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA) (Bonita Springs). A historic, intimate attraction where kids can see rescued birds, reptiles, and lush botanical displays. ($$)

**Evening:** Relaxed dinner at a local family diner.

## Day 3: Beach Relaxation & Local Flavor
**Morning:** [Lovers Key State Park](https://maps.google.com/?cid=7814821748399472511&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA). A quieter, more natural beach experience. Take the tram to the beach and look for shells or go for a gentle swim. ($)

**Afternoon:** [Calusa Nature Center & Planetarium](https://maps.google.com/?cid=17957732074053457904&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA). Perfect for a change of pace; visit the butterfly house and catch a planetarium show. ($)

**Evening:** Farewell dinner at a local favorite.

## Practical tips
*   **Sun & Heat:** Even in March, the Florida sun is strong. Wear hats, sunglasses, and high-SPF sunscreen. Drink plenty of water.
*   **Red Tide:** Before heading to the beach, check the [Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC) Red Tide report](https://myfwc.com/research/redtide/statewide/) to ensure water quality is good.
*   **Traffic:** Traffic can be heavy, especially around bridges to the islands (Sanibel/Fort Myers Beach). Plan to leave early in the morning.
*   **Parking:** Many beach parks charge a parking fee; bring a credit card.
*   **Hurricane Season:** While March is outside of hurricane season (June–Nov), always monitor local news if you see storm alerts.
