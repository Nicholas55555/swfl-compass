> **Prompt** (student): How do I get from FGCU to downtown Fort Myers without a car?

Getting from FGCU to downtown Fort Myers without a car is primarily handled by **LeeTran**, the county's public bus system.

### How to get there:
*   **Primary Route:** You will typically take the **Route 600** bus, which connects the FGCU campus (usually departing from the Student Union/FGCU Blvd N area) to the Rosa Parks Transportation Center in downtown Fort Myers.
*   **Trip Planning:** Because schedules can change, it is highly recommended to use a real-time transit app to plan your specific departure time:
    *   **[LeeTran Trip Planner](https://www.leegov.com/leetran):** The official county site for schedules and maps.
    *   **[Transit App](https://transitapp.com/):** Highly recommended for real-time bus tracking and arrival times.
    *   **[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps):** You can set your destination to "Downtown Fort Myers" and select the "Transit" (bus icon) option for step-by-step directions.

### Tips for Students:
*   **Student ID:** Always carry your FGCU Eagle ID. While LeeTran fares are generally low (around $1.50–$2.00 per ride), check if there are any current student discount programs or semester pass options available through the university.
*   **Timing:** Buses run on a set schedule, so check the app before you head to the stop to avoid long wait times in the heat.
*   **Safety:** The Rosa Parks Transportation Center is the main hub downtown. It is well-lit, but as with any city travel, be aware of your surroundings, especially if you are returning to campus late at night.
