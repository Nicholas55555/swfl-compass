# Summary: Red Tide Current Status | FWC

> **Source:** <https://myfwc.com/research/redtide/statewide/>

As of the October 30, 2026, update, *Karenia brevis* (the red tide organism) was not observed in Southwest Florida. Current red tide activity is limited to Northwest Florida.

### Key points
*   **Southwest Florida Status:** No red tide was detected in the region over the past week.
*   **Northwest Florida Status:** Bloom concentrations were observed in Bay and Gulf counties, with reports of associated fish kills and respiratory irritation in Gulf County.
*   **Monitoring:** FWC updates its daily sampling map at 5 p.m. and issues formal status reports every Friday afternoon.
